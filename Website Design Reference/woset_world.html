  • bonNaG

    Pronunciation

    Bon-neg

    What We Know

    Talented chef. Great sense of smell and taste. The tallest Woset resident. A no-nonsense fellow.

    Bonnag is a talented chef that enjoys nourishing the other Wosets with encouragement and filling their bellies with delicious foods. He is often out in the garden learning about vegetables with Ector or in the Forest learning about magical ingredients from Doona. He often uses his height and long arms to help Ramsey get down some of the too high to reach Noms found at the top of the Nom Nom tree.

    What We Know

    Unimaginably thoughtful. Quite but confident. Kind.

    Breata is a beautiful gentle soul that enjoys to sit and appreciate the wonders of Woset world. She’s confident in who she is but humbled by the beauty of nature.   • CoLbY Biorn

    Pronunciation

    Kol-Bee
    Bee-yawn

    What We Know

    Colby — Shy. Gentle. Feeling of loneliness.

    Biorn — Colby’s mysterious shadow. Always attached to colby and causing trouble.

    Colby is a very gentle and timid soul that prefers to be alone. Unknowingly he often finds himself in trouble without completly understanding the reason why. This can leave Colby feeling sad and alone. But, he is never alone, he has a mischievous shadow named Biorn that follows him everywhere he goes and causes trouble. Sometimes making Colby feel embarrased or ashamed. With the help of his friends, Colby can get a feeling confidence, then he can trap Biorn in his hat.

  • CoSsot

    Pronunciation

    Coz-Ot

    What We Know

    Always banging the drums for others. Sister of Greeba. Entertaining. Funny. Wise.

    Cossot is a musician, but not in the normal sense. She gathers things from Woset world, whatever she can find and can turn them into instruments to play. Reeds near a pond become great string instruments, a big tree makes a mighty drum and some flowers that grow in Woset make for fantastic trumpets. She loves to play for others, it brings her the most joy when her music brings people joy.

    Creative Tool of Choice

    Cossot's Tools Sharpening Instrument

  • EcToR

    Pronunciation

    Ek-Tor

    What We Know

    Woset’s oldest resident. Wise. Thoughtful. Slow walker, but still quick witted. Humble teacher.

    Ector has lived a long and full life. In fact, nobody knows for certain how old Ector really is. Rumors are that he has lived for hundreds (if not thousands) of years. He has done a lot of things in his long life and he doesn’t often speak about these things. But every now and then when people need it; he will tell them a story or give them some wise advice to inspire them.

  • GreEba

    Pronunciation

    Gree-Buh

    What We Know

    Always working on new projects. Proactive. Creative. Sister of Cossot. Knowlegable about creative tools.

    Greeba is the most creative resident of Woset. She loves making things. Often making her own unique creative tools from scratch. She is always helping the other residents finish their projects (sometimes at the expense of her own projects). Greeba is very energetic and easily gets fixated on things. When chatting with Greeba her mind will wander away from the conversation, always thinking about her creative projects.

  • JoneE

    Pronunciation

    Joe-Knee

    What We Know

    Graceful. Eloquent. Astute.

    Jonee is the smartest Woset - she can easily be the leader when she needs to - but prefers not to be. Despite her height, she never looks down on anyone. Actually, she enjoys her height because it means she’s that much closer to the stars. You’ll often find her researching all the bugs that live in Woset. Studying the different kinds of trees and the sky like a scientist. You can find her sometimes in the forest trying to learn more about Doona’s magic. She’s your go-to if you need to know anything about anything.

    Jonee's Well Rounded Markers

  • juaN

    Pronunciation

    Jew-an

    What We Know

    Busy. Small. Talented. And did we mention busy?

    Everything looks big compared to Juan. Step by step by step by step by step by step (He has six legs after all) he can solve any problem he sets his busy mind too. No one knows much about Juan, he’s just always been around and no one knows where he goes when he’s not around. He can fix, repair, and build anything. In fact, most of the unfamiliar (to you and me at least) structures in Woset were built by Juan.

  • neSsA

    Pronunciation

    Ness-ah

    What We Know

    Painfully shy (without the mask). Very sociable (With the mask). Always wears a mask, always. Incredibly kind. Un- conditionally helpful to her friends.

    Nessa is incredibly shy. So much so, that no one has ac- tually ever seen her real face. She used to never leave her house, but one day Greeba came by and slid this horse mask under her door and Nessa loved it. Now, she can leave the house with confidence and join in with the other residents of Woset. The mask means she can’t use her arms and hands very well, but that’s ok, the others help her find a way to be involved.

  • RamSeY

    Pronunciation

    Ram-See

    What We Know

    Content. Happy and helpful. The biggest of all Woset residents.

    Ramsey is largely misunderstood. His size might be intimidating but actually has the soul of Woset that would go around doing something to help everyone without being asked, his extremely generous and never asks for anything in return. Except maybe the occasional Nom fruit, which he welcomes with a smile. Ramsey also makes a great vehicle when Ector is tired or needs to get around quicker. He helps all the Wosets reach things and move big things out of the way. Despite his size, he’s probably the most gentle of the Wosets.
